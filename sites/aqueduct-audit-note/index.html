Water route report

Three dry beds share one broken morning route.

A small audit of the north garden channel found that the plants are not failing from heat alone. The first repair is a route problem: water reaches the stone trough, then disappears before the shaded beds.

Signal Leak path, dry beds, repair route
Note

The visible damage is plant stress, but the cause is a missing handoff.

The first trough fills normally, yet the lower run stays dry by late morning. Soil near the brass marker is damp, while the third bed is powder dry. That pattern points to a small leak or blocked gate between the marker and the shaded run.

The useful move is not a full garden rebuild. Mark the handoff, clear the gate, and run one seven-day water check before adding new planters or changing the planting schedule.
